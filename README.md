# 🦋 Dr. Faizan Siddiqui & Sayma — Luxury Digital Wedding Invitation

A luxury digital wedding invitation featuring a video intro layer with opening doors, ultra-smooth slow sand dissolve transitions, live dynamic countdown timer, wedding events timeline, and WhatsApp blessings & RSVP.

## ✨ Features

- **Opening Gatefold Doors**: Realistic intro video revealing the invitation with interactive pearl tap button.
- **5-Part Centered Snapping Sections**:
  1. **Part 1**: Arabic Bismillah (`بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`), Couple Names (**Dr. Faizan Siddiqui & Sayma**), and invitation greeting.
  2. **Part 2**: Quranic Ayah (Surah Ar-Rum 30:21), Wedding Date (**15 November 2026**), Google Calendar integration, live dynamic countdown timer, and couple story.
  3. **Part 3**: Wedding Events Schedule (Mandha/Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Barat & Wedding, Reception) and Our Journey milestones.
  4. **Part 4**: Venue (**White Palace**), interactive Google Map, phone inquiries (`+91-8377978651`), and in-theme RSVP form.
  5. **Part 5**: Warm Wishes & Duas with direct WhatsApp transmission, and closing Alhamdulillah (`ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰهِ`) footer.
- **Ambient Sand Particle Physics**: Floating golden sand particles gently drifting across the viewport.
- **Audio Control**: Floating headphone widget with soundtrack playback and live animated sound bars.
- **Single-File Configuration**: All couple information, events, dates, and phone numbers are easily editable in `config.js`.

## 🚀 Live Demo on GitHub Pages
